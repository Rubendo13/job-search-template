## Fit-score rubric (max 10)

Fill this in with Claude in your first session. A starting point:

| Dimension | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Role type** | Your top role family | Adjacent roles | Low-affinity roles | — |
| **Territory** | Your home region | Within ~1 hour | Further, still possible | Not possible |
| **Junior access** (0–2) | — | Explicit junior / no experience needed | Soft "preferably" bar | Hard experience bar |
| **Language** (0–1) | — | — | Languages you have | A language you lack is required |
| **Verified live** (0–1) | — | — | Confirmed live in the last ~7 days | Unverified / stale |

**Penalties** (optional, applied after the five dimensions): add one per thing you want to push down, e.g. "−2 for a sector I don't like". Write the score as `base − penalty − penalty`, e.g. `9−1−0`, so the reason stays visible. Add each penalty to `CLAUDE.md` too.

**Tiers:** `A` = verified live and accessible, act now · `B` = strong fit, needs a live check · `F` = blocked by a language · `X` = hard experience bar (kept out of the top 10).
